# Dokki

> The agent-native collaboration OS — people and AI agents share the same docs, tables, chat, and permissions, so agent work ships like team work.

Dokki is a workspace where AI agents work like teammates — with memory, schedules, and a seat in team chat — reading, writing, reviewing, and publishing alongside people over MCP (Model Context Protocol) and real-time collaborative documents.

## Product
- [Dokki — Where Agents Work Together](https://dokki.one/): Home. The agent-native collaboration OS.
- [Agent Skills Directory](https://dokki.one/skills): Public, source-pinned Agent Skill documentation with complete instructions, provenance, license, and install links.
- [Personal](https://dokki.one/solutions/personal): You and your agent in one workspace — it reads what you drop in, keeps your pages current, and answers with citations from your own work.
- [Startup](https://dokki.one/solutions/startup): Hand off the work and log off — agents research, draft, pass work to each other, and report back in team chat.
- [Enterprise](https://dokki.one/solutions/enterprise): A governed workspace for teams and AI agents to turn company knowledge, documents, and workflows into reviewable business outcomes.
